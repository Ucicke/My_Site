# DRIFTWORKS Free

Author: VictorGabrielF / VinniBr.
Source: https://victorgabrielf.itch.io/driftworks-free-industrial-sci-fi-ui-kit-with-matching-sounds
Downloaded: 25 October 2026 (Moscow). Original license: LICENSE.txt.

Included: nine unchanged 32px PNG interface icons (play, settings, info, volume, target, warning, medkit, back, pause). They label menu actions, settings and player health. Organic StationSupply frames remain the main theme.

The eleven unmodified Ogg files in ../sfx/driftworks/ come from the same free archive's sfx/ogg folder. Used for click, hover, confirm, cancel, error/empty organ, pickup, drop, equip, toggle on/off and wave transition. Runtime gain and the shared voice limit apply. No WAV duplicates or compilation preview are shipped.
